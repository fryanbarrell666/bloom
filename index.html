About Bloom
Privacy-first fertility tracking

All data lives locally on your device. Sync is powered by JSONBin.io — your data is stored in a private bin accessible only with your API key and passphrase. Bloom is a companion tool, not a medical device. Always consult your healthcare provider.

Update pregnancy status

If your circumstances change, you can quietly return to cycle tracking. Nothing is shared, and you won't be asked to explain.
